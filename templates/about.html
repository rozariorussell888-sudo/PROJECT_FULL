OUR STORY

Learning becomes better
when we share.

ResourceLoop is a student-focused platform designed to make educational resources easier to discover, share and access.

Whether it is a textbook, class notes, previous exam questions, course materials or useful equipment, students can connect with others who have the resources they need.

◎
OUR MISSION

Make learning accessible.

To help students share useful academic resources, reduce unnecessary barriers and support one another throughout their educational journey.

✦
OUR VISION

A more connected campus.

To build a supportive student community where knowledge moves freely, resources are valued, and every student has more opportunities to learn.
